# Presentation Script (≈1 minute)

Good morning everyone.

Our project is **Electronic Repair Hub**, a digital platform that makes electronic repair easier, faster and more convenient.

When a device stops working, customers usually have to search for a shop, travel there, explain the problem and wait. This is time-consuming and inconvenient.

Our solution moves this process online. A customer selects their device, describes the problem and submits a repair request. A technician handles it, and the status moves from requested to accepted, in progress, repaired and completed.

Technically, the app has a frontend (the interface), a Node.js backend (logic and request processing, with Firebase Authentication) and a database (customers, requests, technicians, status).

Our objective is to organise the repair process digitally and connect customers with technicians conveniently.

In future we can add online payments, live tracking, notifications, ratings, digital invoices and AI-based diagnosis.

Thank you.
